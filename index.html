About

I work the full stack of computing — from gates to dashboards. Computer Engineering graduate (B.Sc., University of Saskatchewan, 2024), now completing a second B.Sc. in Computer Science — Computer Systems specialization (expected Apr 2027).

One week it's a UVM testbench in SystemVerilog and RTL on Intel/Altera FPGAs. The next it's a Raspberry Pi or ESP32 build, a Python data pipeline, a full-stack web app in JavaScript and Docker, or an Android app in Kotlin. The layer changes; the engineering doesn't.

Lately that range extends into AI/ML and analytics — building data dashboards, integrating LLMs into real workflows, and turning raw telemetry into decisions. Hardware taught me to debug at the signal level; software taught me to ship. I use both.
